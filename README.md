# Linden

**Read your skin.**

An early design prototype for Linden, a skin reading and routine app.

[View the prototype](https://sonum-library.github.io/linden/)

---

This is a **visual prototype**, not a working application. It shows the
intended look, feel, motion and screen structure only. Nothing you see is
functional yet, and no data is collected or stored.

## The idea

Linden reads your skin rather than selling to you. It is built around three
things most skincare tools get wrong:

- **Your shelf comes first.** Before recommending anything, it checks what you
  already own, whether two products do the same job, and whether a new one
  clashes with your current plan.
- **Progress has to be honest.** Photo comparisons only count when the lighting,
  angle and distance match, and the app says so when they do not.
- **Sequence and quantity matter.** Most routines fail on order, dose and timing
  rather than on the formulas themselves.

## Planned features

- Photo readings measured zone by zone, with an integrity check on every comparison
- Ingredient conflict flags, correct AM and PM ordering, and phase-in schedules for new actives
- A shelf with opened dates, expiry tracking and usage-rate reminders
- Routine modes for barrier repair, breakouts, travel, post-workout and pre-event
- A skin diary, spoken or typed, filed into trackable signals
- A dermatologist-ready export of concerns, products, changes and outcomes

## Design

Periwinkle gradient mesh with white cards. Instrument Serif for display type
with italic emphasis, Inter for labels and data. The swan is the signature: it
glides through the background, parts the water on opening, and gives the photo
archive its name.
